Before Posting

Keep the haven generous.

  • Share original books, chapters, excerpts, or serial updates.
  • Select the genre, category, and tags that best help readers find your story.
  • Add a profile picture so the Authors page can show your author card.
  • Use a clear book title, chapter title, or series name.
  • Read other authors with care and respect.
