Prism Haven

Prism Haven is an inclusive digital storytelling platform built to empower writers and celebrate diverse voices.

Our mission is to create a safe, welcoming space where LGBTQIA+ creators and allies can share stories without having to change who they are to fit traditional publishing expectations. Whether you’re an experienced author or writing your very first novel, Prism Haven is designed to help your creativity flourish.

We believe every story deserves to be told, every creator deserves to be valued, and every reader deserves to see themselves reflected in the stories they love.

Vision

To build the world’s most welcoming home for diverse storytelling—a place where creators are empowered, communities are built through stories, and every voice belongs.

Mission

* Empower independent authors by giving them a platform to publish and grow.
* Celebrate LGBTQIA+ stories and underrepresented voices.
* Foster a supportive community built on creativity, respect, and inclusion.
* Create opportunities for writers to earn from their work while maintaining ownership of their stories.
* Connect readers with authentic stories they may not find elsewhere.

Planned Features

* 📚 Story publishing and reading platform
* 👤 Author profiles and portfolios
* ❤️ Reader engagement through comments, ratings, and follows
* 💰 Monetization opportunities for creators
* 🔍 Genre and tag discovery
* 🌈 Dedicated support for diverse and inclusive storytelling
* 📱 Responsive web experience with future mobile support

Core Values

* Inclusion — Every story belongs.
* Creativity — Imagination has no limits.
* Community — Writers and readers grow together.
* Authenticity — Tell the stories only you can tell.
* Empowerment — Helping creators turn passion into opportunity.

“Where Every Story Belongs.”
